Who I Am

The Story
Behind the Data

I relocated to the UK from Nigeria in 2023, bringing with me over two years of real-world experience as a Data Analyst at Reddington Hospital in Lagos. I first picked up R during my actuarial science degree at UNILAG, set it down, and returned to it with a completely different mission — to build tools that actually help people.

That mission became clear when I started asking questions the data could answer. Why are house prices in the North West growing faster than London? Why does it take women an average of two years to get a PCOS diagnosis? I do not just analyse data. I build tools that help people understand and act on it.

By day I work as a Strategic Data Analyst at the Rural Payments Agency, building automated reporting pipelines and Power BI dashboards that inform government decision-making. I hold a BSc in Actuarial Science and Insurance from the University of Lagos, an MSc in Applied Data Science from Teesside University with distinction, and a PL-300 Microsoft Power BI certification.

I am currently documenting my full analytics journey publicly on LinkedIn and YouTube, one real project at a time. Every chart, every model, every line of code is shared so others can learn alongside me.

My Journey

Timeline

2017–2021

BSc Actuarial Science & Insurance · University of Lagos, Nigeria

Graduated from UNILAG with a Bachelor of Science in Actuarial Science and Insurance — a rigorous quantitative degree covering probability theory, statistical modelling, financial mathematics, and risk analysis. This foundation laid the mathematical groundwork for everything that followed in data science.

2021–2023

Data Analyst · Reddington Hospital, Lagos, Nigeria

Worked as a Data Analyst at Reddington Hospital in Lagos — one of Nigeria's leading private hospitals. Gained hands-on experience in health data analysis, reporting, and supporting clinical decision-making with data. This role cemented a passion for health data and laid the foundation for later work in healthcare machine learning.

2023

Relocated to the UK · Started MSc at Teesside University

Moved from Nigeria to the North East of England. Began MSc in Applied Data Science at Teesside University, diving deep into machine learning, statistical modelling, and Python.

2023–2025

MSc Applied Data Science · Teesside University · Distinction

Completed a full Master of Science in Applied Data Science, graduating with distinction. Dissertation focused on predicting diabetic patient readmission using XGBoost, CatBoost, and SHAP interpretability across 100K+ patient records from 130 US hospitals.

2025

PL-300 Certified · UK House Price Shiny App · PCOS Health Companion

Achieved Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst certification. Built and deployed two end-to-end R Shiny applications — an interactive UK house price explorer and a free PCOS health companion tool for women across the UK.

2026

Strategic Data Analyst · Rural Payments Agency · 6-Month Roadmap

Working as a Strategic Data Analyst at the Rural Payments Agency, building automated Power BI pipelines that inform government decision-making. Launched a structured 6-month self-development roadmap covering SQL, Excel, Python, and ML — building and sharing real projects publicly.
